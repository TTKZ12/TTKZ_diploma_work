## Recorded generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide photographic hero background for TTKZ, a table tennis community platform in Kazakhstan. Create a cinematic professional sports photograph in landscape 16:9. A young adult table tennis player in a plain dark sports shirt returning a shot on the RIGHT HALF of the frame, positioned behind a full-size deep blue table tennis table with a realistic net, in a dark indoor training arena. Camera is low close to table height, dramatic overhead white lights, dark charcoal and deep navy colors with restrained lime accent on the shirt. Crisp realistic anatomy and correct table tennis racket, ball, table and net. The left 45 percent is intentionally very dark, soft, quiet negative space suitable for a large white headline overlay. Editorial sports photography with slight film grain and believable motion, no artificial neon, no excessive gradients. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no audience claims, no signage. Used as fictional atmosphere, not a real venue.
